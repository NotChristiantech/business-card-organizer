# The weekly run

One session a week, about 45 minutes. Everything else is ten seconds at a time.

---

## Monday — the writing session (~45 min)

**Start it**

- [ ] `cd linkedin-amplifier && npm run dev` → open http://localhost:3001

**Pick the material (5 min)**

- [ ] **Ideas** → read the top of the list, sorted by score
- [ ] Pick **3**. At least one that is a lesson you learned, not news you are announcing.
- [ ] Archive anything that has sat there three weeks. It was not good enough.

**Write (20 min)**

- [ ] For each idea: **Write posts**
- [ ] Leave the format selection alone unless you have a reason. The Reflection is weighted highest on purpose — it is your best writing and your rarest.
- [ ] Generate, then read the **model's self-critique** on each variant before you read the post. It tells you which one is stretching.
- [ ] Keep one variant. Discard the rest without guilt.

**Make it yours (15 min)** — this is the step that matters

- [ ] Read it out loud. If you would not say it, cut it.
- [ ] Is there a number where there is currently an adjective?
- [ ] Is anyone credited who taught you this?
- [ ] Is there exactly **one** ask, and **one** link, appearing **once**?
- [ ] Does it end on a turn — a reversal, or the credit going elsewhere?
- [ ] **Read it back once more for typos.** Thirty seconds. Your most-viewed post last quarter had one in it.
- [ ] Approve → it lands in **Queue**

**Schedule (5 min)**

- [ ] **Queue** → **Copy for LinkedIn**
- [ ] Paste into the LinkedIn composer
- [ ] Click the **clock icon** → pick date and time → Next → Schedule
- [ ] Repeat for each post
- [ ] Close the laptop. LinkedIn publishes whether your machine is on or not.

---

## During the week — ten seconds at a time

- [ ] Thought worth keeping? **Ideas → Quick capture → Save raw.** No model call, instant.
- [ ] Good meeting? **Ideas → Meetings → Mine.** Do it the same day while you still know which part mattered.
- [ ] Someone did great work for you? Write **The Boost** and post it same-day. Two lines, their name, their number. It costs nothing and it is the most characteristic thing you publish.

---

## Friday — close the loop (10 min)

- [ ] **Performance** → log numbers for anything that went out this week
- [ ] Impressions, reactions, comments, reposts — and **inbound conversations**, which is the only one that really matters
- [ ] Once a month: **Reweight formats**, then **What's working?**

Without this step the format weights never learn anything and the whole feedback loop is decoration.

---

## Cadence targets

| | Target |
|---|---|
| Posts per week | 3–4 |
| Reflections per week | **At least 1** |
| Program promos per week | No more than 1 |
| Words per post | Under 200 |

The Reflection target is the whole point. One per quarter was the largest single gap in your style analysis.

---

## Before you hit publish — the never list

- No "Call to action:" printed in the body
- No unicode bold letterforms (they break screen readers)
- No link twice
- No benefits list where one named business and one number would do
- No "we" and "I" mixed in the same post
- **No rate, yield, return, term, or minimum investment.** Ever, in any post. Explain what an instrument is and invite people to a session. Never say what it pays.

---

## When something breaks

**Drafts sound generic** → the voice profile is not active. Check the Voice page; re-run `npm run seed:voice`.

**Wrong formats offered** → re-run `npm run seed:formats`. Built-ins deactivate by default.

**A draft carries a COMPLIANCE FLAG** → it combined a financial term with investment language. Do not publish it as-is. Rewrite or discard.

**Nothing in Ideas** → you have not mined a meeting in too long. That is the input, and it is the one nobody else has.
