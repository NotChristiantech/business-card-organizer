## Why this decline is the most informative one in the archive

Two applications, one funder, one period, opposite outcomes. Vancity said yes to
capital and no to research. That is a clean read on where ACBN is credible with
this funder and where it is not — worth more than either result alone, and the
kind of comparison you almost never get.

The plausible reading is that ACBN presents strongly as a housing *operator* —
175 Springbank is in construction with CMHC funds flowing — and weakly as a
research *institution*, having no published research capability on record. If
so, the lesson generalises: pursue delivery and capital funding with this funder,
and partner with an academic institution for anything research-shaped rather
than applying alone. Sheridan College is already a research partner
(`library/partners.md`).

**Confirm this reading before acting on it.** It is inference from two data
points, and Vancity can tell us the actual reason if asked.
