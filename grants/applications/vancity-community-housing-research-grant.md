# Vancity — Building the Blueprint

*A Funding and Community Bond Playbook for Black-led Organizations.* Tracked
earlier under the working title "Community Housing Grant for Research", which
was wrong — this is a capacity-building proposal, not housing research.

Working notes in Drive: "Vancity Community Foundaiton Grant Notes"

## Outcome — DECLINED, on budget and scope, not merit

From Jenn McGinn, Manager of the Affordable Community Housing Program:

> "While the project aligns with important capacity-building objectives, it falls
> outside the scope of the projects we are currently funding. At this time, our
> grant funding is focused on **pre-development activities**, and our
> **capacity-building grant allocation for this year has already been fully
> committed**."

**This is the most favourable kind of decline there is.** Not eligibility, not
quality, not fit with ACBN — the allocation was simply spent, and the proposal
sat outside the window their grant money is currently pointed at. They also went
out of their way to note the pre-development loan just approved for Springbank.

## Two hypotheses this kills

Both were recorded in this repo, and both were wrong. Worth keeping, because
each would have sent ACBN off fixing something that was never broken:

1. **"ACBN reads as a housing operator, not a research institution."** No —
   Vancity never assessed research credibility. They ran out of
   capacity-building money.
2. **"Foundations decline ACBN because it is not a qualified donee."** Also no.
   Charitable status is a genuine constraint in general
   (`../library/eligibility.md`), but it played no part here.

The method lesson is the durable one: two plausible, mutually exclusive theories
were built from the same two data points, and a single email settled it. **Ask
the funder before theorising.**

## They left the door open — twice

> "Once the playbook has been developed, we would be happy to connect you with
> Black-led organizations in British Columbia that may find this resource
> valuable and relevant to their work."

A distribution offer, and worth more than it looks. It gives ACBN a reason to
return to Vancity with something finished rather than a request, and extends the
playbook into a province where ACBN has no presence.
