## Correction to the original record

The ACBN Grant Tracker lists this under **ACOA**, and this record was first
created that way — with a blocking note that ACOA funds Atlantic Canada and ACBN
is Ontario-based, so it was probably ineligible.

Drive says otherwise: the working folder is **"FEDDEV RAII - Stream 2 Non
Profit"**, created 2024-12-20. RAII is a federal program delivered through the
regional development agencies, so the Ontario route through FedDev is the
applicable one and the eligibility concern was misdirected.

Refiled under FedDev. Confirm which agency the application actually went to —
if it genuinely went to ACOA, that eligibility question comes back.

## Fit

Stream 2 is the not-for-profit stream, which is the right door for ACBN. The
natural concept is AI adoption support for Black-owned small businesses — it
sits inside the Convening/technology pillar, and the survey finding that **70% of
ACBN's founders are in traditional low-tech companies** is a ready-made needs
statement for exactly this program.

FedDev is also ACBN's strongest funder relationship (BEP Ecosystem Fund,
$1,315,000; Community Revitalization Fund, $500,000), which materially improves
the odds here.
