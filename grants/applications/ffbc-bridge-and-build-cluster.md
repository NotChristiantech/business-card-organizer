## Why this is the most instructive record in the archive

Five applications to one funder in one cycle is a natural experiment. If some
were funded and some were not, the difference between them is the closest thing
ACBN has to a controlled read on what FFBC actually rewards — worth more than
any amount of general advice about grant writing.

Recovering the per-organization outcomes is the highest-value backfill task in
this whole system.
