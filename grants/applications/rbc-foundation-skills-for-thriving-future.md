## The rolling-deadline trap

"Rolling" means there is no forcing function, and applications without a
forcing function do not get written. **Set a self-imposed submission date and
treat it as real**, or this row will still read `prospect` a year from now.

## Positioning

Corporate foundation — brand alignment, visible impact, participant stories and
photography carry weight here in a way they don't with ESDC. A warm introduction
matters more than at government funders; RBC's Black Entrepreneurship banking
program is a plausible route in.
