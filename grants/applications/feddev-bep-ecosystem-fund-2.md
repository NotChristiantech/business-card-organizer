# FedDev — BEP Ecosystem Fund 2.0

## Why this is the top priority in the pipeline

The first BEP Ecosystem Fund award was **$1,315,000** — the largest in ACBN's
history and roughly half of all funding the organization has ever received. A
renewal is worth more than every other open application combined, and a lapse
would be the single largest revenue event ACBN could experience.

Nothing else in this pipeline deserves attention ahead of it.
