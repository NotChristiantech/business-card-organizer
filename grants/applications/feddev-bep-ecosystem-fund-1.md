FedDev to increase its investment to $2,000,000 in 2026.

## Why this record matters even though it is closed

The reported results from this award are the **single most valuable missing data
in the system**. Every competitively scored application ACBN loses is lost on
outcome evidence, and this project committed to a specific indicator set —
entrepreneurs served, unregistered-to-registered conversions, revenue growth,
satisfaction rates.

Whatever was reported to FedDev is exactly the evidence the rest of the
portfolio lacks.
