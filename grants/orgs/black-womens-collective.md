# Black Women's Collective

Proposed **co-applicant** — and potentially lead applicant — for the WAGE
Women's Economic and Leadership Opportunities Fund 2026
(`../applications/wage-womens-economic-leadership-2026.md`).

## Why this partnership matters strategically

WAGE **prioritizes women's organizations**. ACBN is a Black business network with
a majority-women constituency, which is not the same thing and is a real
disadvantage as lead applicant.

A Black women's organization in the lead converts ACBN's weakest positioning into
its strongest: BWC brings the mandate, ACBN brings a $4.8M track record, a
working Black-led lending model, and primary research on the affected population.

## Confirm urgently — these gate the application

WAGE requires every applicant to be a Canadian not-for-profit, **legally
constituted, with proof of incorporation or creation under an Act**, and to have
**experience advancing equality for women**.

- [ ] Legal name exactly as incorporated
- [ ] Proof of incorporation — jurisdiction, number, date
- [ ] Province and geographic reach (decides local vs. regional vs. interprovincial)
- [ ] Documented experience advancing equality for women
- [ ] Leadership and signing authority
- [ ] Whether BWC will lead, co-apply, or partner
- [ ] Capacity to hold or share a multi-year federal agreement

If BWC is not incorporated, it cannot be an applicant — but can still be a named
delivery partner. Establish this before building the application around them.
