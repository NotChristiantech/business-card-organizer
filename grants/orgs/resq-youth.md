# ResQ Youth International

Long-standing client relationship. ACBN secured **$30,000** through the
Investment Readiness Program on their behalf.

## WAGE co-applicant (confirmed willing, 2026-09-04)

Joining the Women's Economic and Leadership Opportunities Fund application
(`../applications/wage-womens-economic-leadership-2026.md`) alongside ACBN and
New Life Project.

**What ResQ brings that matters here:**

- **Woman-founded and woman-led.** Abigail Hamilton is Founder and Executive
  Director. This is the closest thing in the consortium to the women's-
  organization standing WAGE prioritizes.
- **Charitable status**, which appears in Abigail's email signature — a stronger
  compliance signal than ACBN's own non-charitable structure.
- **Youth reach**, which adds *age* as an intersecting factor to the GBA Plus
  analysis and brings young Black women entrepreneurs into scope.
- **Documented prior delivery with ACBN** — the $30,000 IRP award.

**Be honest about the limit:** ResQ is a *youth* organization that is
woman-led, not a *women's* organization. That partially closes the gap left when
Black Women's Collective turned out to be for-profit; it does not close it
entirely. Do not describe ResQ as a women's organization in the application —
overstating a partner's identity is exactly the kind of claim a reviewer checks.

**Worth establishing quickly:** does ResQ run programming specifically for girls
or young women? If so, that materially strengthens the consortium's claim to
"experience advancing equality for women", which WAGE requires of applicants.

## ⚠ Capacity — a real delivery risk to weigh

Abigail's response to the Peel Region announcements was that several align with
their mandate and she wants to **"apply for all of them"**. ResQ is also pursuing
the Law Foundation and a responsive call.

A co-applicant on a 48–60 month federal agreement needs sustained capacity, and
ResQ is currently spreading itself across several applications at once. Have the
conversation before naming them: what will they actually be resourced to deliver
here, and what happens to it if the Peel applications land?

This is not a reason to exclude them. It is a reason to define their work package
narrowly and fund it properly.
