# New Life Project

## History with ACBN

ACBN secured **$21,000** for New Life Project through the Supporting Black
Canadian Communities Initiative — one of three organizations in ACBN's
funding-for-others track record (`../library/track-record.md`).

That matters here: it is documented evidence that ACBN and New Life have already
delivered a federal grant relationship together. A WAGE reviewer assessing
partnership risk is asking exactly that question.

## Proposed role

Co-applicant or delivery partner on the WAGE Women's Economic and Leadership
Opportunities Fund 2026
(`../applications/wage-womens-economic-leadership-2026.md`).
