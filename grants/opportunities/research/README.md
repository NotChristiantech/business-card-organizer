# Funder research

Proactive prospecting — funders and calls ACBN would qualify for but hasn't
found yet. Distinct from `inbox.md`, which handles what arrives on its own.

## Method

1. Start from the profile: Black-led Ontario nonprofit, entrepreneurship +
   community economic development + housing, prior awards $500–$1.3M, national
   reach through 40+ partners.
2. Search the sources below for open calls.
3. Screen against the questions in `../README.md`.
4. Write a dated findings file: `YYYY-MM-DD-<theme>.md`.
5. Promote anything worth pursuing into `../inbox.md` with a verdict.

## Sources to sweep

**Federal** — Grants and Contributions Online Services (ESDC), ISED and the
Black Entrepreneurship Program, regional development agencies (FedDev, ACOA,
PrairiesCan, PacifiCan), Public Safety Canada, Canadian Heritage, Housing
Infrastructure and Communities Canada, CMHC.

**Provincial** — Ontario Trillium Foundation, Ontario grant portal, provincial
housing programs.

**Municipal** — Peel Region, City of Brampton, City of Mississauga, City of
Toronto.

**Foundations** — Foundation for Black Communities, Community Foundations of
Canada, Toronto Foundation, Ontario Trillium, Definity Insurance Foundation,
Catherine Donnelly Foundation, McConnell Foundation.

**Corporate** — RBC, TD Ready Commitment, BMO, Scotiabank, CIBC, Alterna
Savings, Desjardins. ACBN already has TD and Alterna history — warm rather than
cold.

**Aggregators** — Pocketed digest, GrantConnect (Imagine Canada), Ontario
Nonprofit Network.

## Standing priorities

Weight prospecting toward these, because they are where ACBN's gaps are:

1. **Multi-year core / operating funding.** ACBN's portfolio is overwhelmingly
   project money, which does not pay for the staff who run the projects.
2. **Renewals of what already works.** BEP Ecosystem Fund, SBCCI, YOF, CSJ. A
   renewal is worth several times a cold application at the same value.
3. **Housing capital.** A live workstream currently thin on funder options.
4. **Anything that funds evaluation infrastructure**, which would close the
   outcome-measurement gap in `library/reach-and-impact.md` and lift every
   subsequent application.
