# Mission and mandate

## Short (50 words)

The Afro-Caribbean Business Network is a Black-led nonprofit based in
Peel Region, Ontario, that helps Black entrepreneurs across Canada start, sustain
and grow their businesses — through direct microloan financing of $500 to
$25,000, capacity-building programs, and a national network of 40+ partner
organizations.

## Medium (150 words)

The Afro-Caribbean Business Network (ACBN) was created to give Black
business owners the resource they were missing: real support to succeed. Running
a business is difficult alone, and Black entrepreneurs in Canada face the
additional barriers of restricted access to capital, thinner professional
networks, and institutions that were not built with them in mind.

ACBN works across four pillars. **Entrepreneurship** — programs and services that
develop the next generation of Black Canadian founders. **Convening** —
connecting the community through technology. **Access to Funding** — direct
microloan capital from $500 to $25,000, alongside the financial literacy needed
to use it well. **Economic Inclusion** — dismantling the economic barriers facing
the underrepresented.

Our database holds 6,000 Black entrepreneurs, 74% of them women. Through 40+
partner organizations, we reach more than 30,000 Black entrepreneurs across
Canada.
