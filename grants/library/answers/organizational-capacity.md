# Organizational capacity

## The core claim

ACBN has received and administered approximately $2.58M in grant funding across
16 awards, scaling from a $500 community grant to a $1,315,000 federal ecosystem
fund. Every award has been managed and reported to the funder's requirements.

## Why this answer usually wins

The capacity question is really a risk question. The reviewer is asking: *if we
give this organization money, will it be spent properly and reported on time?*
Answer the question they're actually asking.

The graduated arc is the argument. An organization that jumped straight to a
$1M ask would be a risk; one that earned its way up through 16 awards has a
documented history at every scale below the current request. Show the ladder.

## Federal reporting credibility

For federal programs, name the federal awards specifically — Investment
Readiness Program, Supporting Black Canadian Communities Initiative, Community
Revitalization Fund, Black Entrepreneurship Program Ecosystem Fund, Canada
Summer Jobs. Federal reporting is its own discipline and prior federal
compliance is the strongest possible signal.

## Intermediary capacity

Where the program funds re-granting, brokering or ecosystem support, add: ACBN
has secured $151,000 for three partner organizations (ResQ Youth International —
$30,000 IRP; Custodia — $100,000 IRP; New Life Project — $21,000 SBCCI), and has
supported additional organizations through the FFBC Bridge and Build Grant. Very
few organizations at ACBN's scale can evidence moving capital to peers.

## Before submitting

Confirm these, which capacity questions almost always require and which are
currently unrecorded (see `reach-and-impact.md`):

- staff FTE count and org chart
