## The partnership model, as sold to FedDev

Worth reusing wholesale — it is a specific, credible answer to "how will you
manage partners", which most applicants answer vaguely:

- All clients managed through a centralised system; partners report to ACBN's
  Brampton head office **weekly**.
- Monthly reporting per partner, quarterly all-partner strategy meetings.
- Each partner carries an individually assigned mandate, not a shared vague one.
- If a partner falls behind, ACBN coaches them; if it is mutually agreed they
  cannot deliver, **ACBN staff take over the work in that region**. Naming the
  failure path is what makes the rest believable.
