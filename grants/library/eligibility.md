# Eligibility constraints

Check these **before** reading a funder's guidelines properly. Each one voids an
application regardless of how well it is written, and each has cost ACBN a
drafting cycle at least once.

## 1. ACBN is not a registered charity

The Articles of Amendment state the corporation operates as a
**"non-charitable not-for-profit corporation."**

| | |
|---|---|
| Registered charity | **No** |
| Qualified donee | **No** |
| Can issue tax receipts | **No** |
| Legal form | Ontario not-for-profit corporation (ONCA), OCN 1987142 |

**Consequences:**

- Ineligible for programs restricted to registered charities or qualified donees
- Many **private and corporate foundations** grant only to qualified donees —
  this bars a large part of the philanthropic market
- **Government programs are generally fine.** Federal and provincial programs
  usually accept incorporated not-for-profits, which is consistent with where
  ACBN's wins are concentrated
- **Loans are unaffected** — lenders do not require donee status

**No confirmed instance yet.** This was suspected in the Vancity case — loan
approved, grant declined — but Vancity confirmed in writing that the decline was
about scope and an exhausted allocation, not eligibility. So the constraint
below is real and structural, but it has not yet been shown to have cost ACBN a
specific grant. Do not assume it explains a decline; ask.

**Workarounds, in order of preference:**

1. **Apply as the qualified donee's partner.** ACBN works with charitable
   organizations that can hold the grant while ACBN delivers.
2. **Trustee arrangement.** There is a `$ - Trustee Services` folder in Drive
   (`1WmUBX1XzYtg6ZglI-LvZnq__II2I2mNV`) that likely documents an existing
   approach — read it before inventing a new one.
3. **Ask the funder.** Some foundations grant to non-charitable nonprofits doing
   charitable-purpose work; the guidelines do not always say so.

- [ ] Audit which past declines were charitable-status related
- [ ] Read the Trustee Services folder and document the arrangement here
- [ ] Decide deliberately whether to pursue charitable registration — it would
      open the foundation market, and the current structure was a choice
