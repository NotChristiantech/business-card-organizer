# Research library

Source folder: **Research Reports — Black Community**
`https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1pZ0_hDgFPNdFQRE9CqbCkTK48r0YXCkf`

The evidence base for needs statements. Until this was ingested,
`answers/community-need.md` carried a checklist of external evidence ACBN
"should be citing but is not" — that gap is now closed. Use `evidence-base.md`
for citable figures and `acbn-own-research.md` for the material ACBN produced
itself, which is worth far more.

## Ingest status

The two ACBN Review documents and the Study Guide have been read and distilled
into `evidence-base.md` and `acbn-own-research.md`. The **PDFs have not been read
directly** — the figures below come from ACBN's own summaries of them. Before
quoting a specific number in a submitted application, open the source PDF and
confirm it. A misquoted statistic attributed to the Senate is not a small error.
