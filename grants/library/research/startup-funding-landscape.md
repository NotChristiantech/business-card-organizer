# Canadian startup funding landscape

A reference on **for-profit** funding: tax credits, non-dilutive programs, debt
and equity. Substantive guidance rather than marketing, though it is published
by a law firm and carries a "legal information, not legal advice" disclaimer.
Verify figures at source before relying on them.

## Why this belongs in a grant library

ACBN itself cannot use most of it — as a not-for-profit it is ineligible for
SR&ED and the rest. It matters for three other reasons.

**1. It is the reference for ACBN's advisory work.** ACBN's microloan clients,
accelerator participants and grant-writing clients are for-profit businesses,
and this is the map of what they can access. `orgs/acbn.md` records that ACBN
"facilitates grant writing and business plan workshops" — this is source
material for that service.

**2. It covers ACBN's own for-profit entities and partners.** Detailing Knights
Canada Inc., Sir Ryan Knight Consulting, and **Black Women's Collective** — which
cannot be a grant applicant on WAGE precisely because it is for-profit — are all
in scope here. Offering BWC this route is a concrete way to give something back
to a partner that cannot share in grant funding.

**3. It documents the gates.** See below.

## Funding types, ranked by what a founder gives up

| Type | Range |
|---|---|
| Refundable tax credits | no equity or repayment |
| Non-repayable grants | $25K – $1M |
| Debt | $25K – $1.15M |
| Friends and family | $10K – $250K |
| Angels | $50K – $1M |
| Venture capital | $1M+ |

## Federal programs

| Program | Amount |
|---|---|
| **SR&ED** | 35% of eligible R&D spending, in cash, on up to $6M/year (Canadian-controlled private corporations) |
| **NRC IRAP** | $50,000 – $1,000,000 |
| **Innovative Solutions Canada** | Phase 1 $150,000 · Phase 2 $1,000,000 |
| **Canada Small Business Financing** | up to $1,150,000 |
| **Futurpreneur** | up to $75,000 |
| **BDC startup financing** | up to $150,000 — requires 12 months operating history |

## Ontario programs

- Interactive Digital Media Tax Credit — **40%** of labour costs
- Ontario Innovation Tax Credit — **8%** refundable on up to $3M
- Business-Research Institute Tax Credit — **20%** refundable
- Intellectual Property Fund — $15,000–$50,000, rising to $250,000–$500,000

**Ontario has no investor tax credit for private companies**, while Saskatchewan
and Manitoba offer 45%, New Brunswick 50%, Nova Scotia 35% (45% for oceantech
and life sciences) and BC 30% refundable. A real disadvantage for Ontario
founders raising private capital, and a legitimate policy point for ACBN's
advocacy work.

## Sequencing advice worth passing on

1. **Claim before you sell** — pursue refundable credits before raising equity
2. **Register before you raise** — get provincial investor credit approval before
   closing a round; there is no retroactive fix
3. **Geography is a decision** — where engineers sit determines provincial credit rates
4. **Document contemporaneously** — claims get examined later

## The connection to the WAGE application

This article is, read a certain way, **a catalogue of the qualification gates**
that the *Qualification Gap* project exists to address
(`../../applications/wage-womens-economic-leadership-2026.md`).

Look at what each program silently requires:

- **SR&ED** — a Canadian-controlled *private corporation*, plus contemporaneous
  R&D documentation
- **Canada Small Business Financing** — delivered through banks, so it inherits
  bank credit adjudication entirely
- **BDC startup financing** — **12 months operating history**
- **Provincial investor credits** — pre-registration before a raise

Now set that against ACBN's own survey: **71% of its founders are unregistered or
sole proprietors.** Not incorporated means no SR&ED and no investor credits. No
bank relationship means no CSBFP. No trading history means no BDC.

The formal funding architecture of this country is built on qualification
thresholds that exclude the majority of ACBN's constituency at the first gate —
and none of those thresholds mention race or gender. That is the *inequity, not
inequality* argument in `../theory-of-change.md`, evidenced program by program.

**This is quotable in WP1 evidence.** Each program is a documented instance of a
neutral rule producing an exclusionary outcome, and the list is public.
