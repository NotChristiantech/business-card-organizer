## Gaps to close

These are the figures funders ask for that we cannot currently answer. Each one
is a question we've had to dodge, and dodging costs points on the rubric.

- [ ] Number of entrepreneurs served per year (distinct from database size —
      reviewers know the difference and will ask)
- [ ] Microloans issued: count, total value, repayment rate. **Repayment rate is
      the highest-leverage missing number in this whole file** — it converts the
      microloan program from an activity into a proven financial instrument.
- [ ] Jobs created or sustained through ACBN-supported businesses
- [ ] Business survival rate of supported entrepreneurs vs. baseline
- [ ] Program completion rates for cohort programs
- [ ] Annual operating budget and staff FTE count
- [ ] Volunteer / mentor count and hours

Ask for these once, record them here, and every future application gets stronger.
