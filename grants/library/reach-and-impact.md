# Reach and impact figures

The numbers ACBN quotes. One place, so two applications never contradict each
other — funders do talk, and a mismatch is a credibility problem.

## Community reach

Two sources, several years apart, and they do not agree. Both are recorded with
dates so a new application never contradicts an old one.

| Metric | Figure | Source | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Black entrepreneurs in ACBN's internal database | 6,000 | E4x deck | ~2026 |
| Female entrepreneurs (share of database) | 74% | E4x deck | ~2026 |
| Founders / CEOs (share of database) | 91% | E4x deck | ~2026 |

| Network of Black businesses | 3,000+ | BEP concept paper | ~2021 |
| Women as share of membership | 65% | BEP concept paper | ~2021 |

**Reconcile before quoting.** 3,500 → 6,000 over roughly five years is credible
growth, and 65% → 74% women is plausible drift. But the two sets are used
interchangeably in ACBN material, and a funder comparing a 2021 application to a
2026 one will notice. Decide which figures are current, date them, and retire
the rest.

## ACBN's own survey — the most under-used asset here

ACBN conducted a survey of **1,014 Black founders** in Peel and the GTA. Findings
quoted in the Ecosystem Fund application:

- **70%** are in traditional service-based, low-tech companies
- **71%** were either unregistered or sole proprietors

That second figure is the sharpest thing in the whole library. It is what made
the argument that Black businesses were excluded from the $962M Regional
Recovery Relief Fund, which restricted support to incorporated firms — a
concrete, documented instance of a neutral rule producing an inequitable
outcome. It is ACBN's own primary research, which almost no applicant of this
size can offer.

Raw results: `https://drive.google.com/file/d/1IhByLhOQc9T00j-GMlAWGgS9g6BB1HWm/view`

Confirm the survey date and whether it can still be cited as current. If it is
ageing, **re-running it is probably the single highest-return investment ACBN
could make in its grant writing** — it is the only evidence in the portfolio that
belongs to ACBN alone.

## External statistics used

Cited in the Ecosystem Fund application to establish the financing gap. Refresh
before reuse; these date from roughly 2021.

- Private equity / VC firms have funded less than **3%** of Black-owned ventures;
  less than **1%** are Black female founders
- **80%** of investment partners at VC firms are White; 3% Latinx, 3% Black (NVCA)
- **77–80%** of venture rounds go to companies with all-White founding teams
- More than 75% of all rounds raised go to all-White founding teams (Kauffman
  Fellows / MaC Venture Capital Report)
- In 2018, 11 African American women raised $1M in VC funding — about **0.2%** of
  all venture funding that year (Amoye Henry, via BetaKit)

## Distinctive claims ACBN can make

From the Ecosystem Fund application. Each is a differentiator, not a
description — verify each is still true before reuse.

- **The only Black-led microloan program in Ontario.** A strong claim; confirm
  it still holds before putting it in writing.
- ACBN operates **its own marketing agency** supporting Black entrepreneurs with
  strategy, campaigns, and a proprietary metrics dashboard.
- ACBN facilitates **grant writing and business plan workshops**, and delivers
  those services directly — the capability this repository documents.
- Procurement support: pairing entrepreneurs with teams that research open bids
  and complete proposals.
- A **research partnership with Sheridan College** on the entrepreneurial needs
  of Black founders.

## Gaps to close

These are the figures funders ask for that we cannot currently answer. Each one
is a question we've had to dodge, and dodging costs points on the rubric.

- [ ] **Actual results from the completed BEP Ecosystem Fund award** against the
      indicators committed to in the application. BEP 2.0 is effectively judged
      on BEP 1.0's reported performance, so this outranks everything else here.
- [ ] Number of entrepreneurs served per year (distinct from database size —
      reviewers know the difference and will ask)
- [ ] Microloans issued: count, total value, repayment rate. **Repayment rate is
      the highest-leverage missing number in this whole file** — it converts the
      microloan program from an activity into a proven financial instrument.
- [ ] Jobs created or sustained through ACBN-supported businesses
- [ ] Business survival rate of supported entrepreneurs vs. baseline
- [ ] Program completion rates for cohort programs
- [ ] Annual operating budget and staff FTE count
- [ ] Volunteer / mentor count and hours

Ask for these once, record them here, and every future application gets stronger.
