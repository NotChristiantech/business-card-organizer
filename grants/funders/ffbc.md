# Foundation for Black Communities (FFBC)

Canada's first philanthropic foundation for and by Black communities. Strategically
the most important funder relationship in this file: aligned by mandate, and ACBN
has run *multiple* organizations through their BIG Bridge and Build Grant.

## Portal notes

SmApply. Each organization needs its own account; there is no multi-org view, so
coordinating a cluster means tracking five separate logins and five separate
deadlines. That coordination burden is the main operational cost of the
cluster-application strategy and is worth budgeting for explicitly.

## How they decide

Community-rooted review — reviewers are from Black communities and know the
sector. Practical consequence: generic "we serve the Black community" language
lands worse here than anywhere else. Be specific, be local, name partners.

## Lessons

- The cluster strategy is a genuine differentiator. It demonstrates ACBN's
  intermediary role in the most concrete way possible.
- FFBC is worth over-investing in relationally, not just transactionally. Their
  calls are the best mandate-fit ACBN has.
