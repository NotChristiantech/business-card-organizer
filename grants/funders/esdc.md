# Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC)

ACBN's most-used funder by volume. Multiple distinct programs share one portal
(Grants and Contributions Online Services, "GCOS"), which is both convenient and
a trap — deadlines are per-program and do not appear together anywhere.

## Programs ACBN engages

## Portal notes

- GCOS at `srv136.services.gc.ca/OSR/pro/` — separate entry paths per program
  (`Project/`, `Grant/`, `GCOS/BusinessIndex`).
- **Keep confirmation numbers.** ESDC's confirmation number is the only reliable
  proof of submission and the only thing they will act on in a status enquiry.
  CSJ 2026's is `A001070969`.
- Submission does not generate a useful acknowledgement email; do not treat
  inbox silence as a problem for ESDC the way you would for a foundation.

## How they decide

Federal assessment against published criteria, scored by program officers. Two
practical consequences:

1. **Mirror the guidelines' vocabulary exactly.** The rubric is written in the
   program's own words and scored clause by clause.
2. **Answer every sub-question separately.** A merged elegant paragraph that
   covers three sub-questions scores lower than three plain ones, because the
   scorer is ticking sub-questions.

## Lessons

- Canada Summer Jobs is the reliable annual base — it recurs, ACBN has completed
  it before (2025 completed, 2026 submitted), and the application is largely
  reusable year to year. Diary it early; the window is short and fixed.
- ESDC programs have real compliance overhead after the award. Budget the
  reporting time when deciding whether to apply.
