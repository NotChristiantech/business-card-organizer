## Positioning

Corporate foundations weight brand alignment and visible impact more heavily
than government funders do. Photographs, participant stories and named
partnerships carry real weight here in a way they do not at ESDC. RBC also runs
a Black Entrepreneurship Program on the banking side — a live relationship
there is useful context for the foundation ask.
