## Open question — check before investing more time

Vancity is a BC-based credit union foundation and its community funding has
historically been BC-focused. ACBN operates from Ontario. **Confirm national
eligibility before drafting**, because this is exactly the kind of geographic
constraint that voids an otherwise strong application.

## Grant vs. loan

Being pursued together. They are different instruments with different risk: the
loan creates a repayment obligation on the balance sheet and needs board
awareness and a servicing plan, not just an application.
