# Women and Gender Equality Canada (WAGE)

New funder — no history with ACBN.

## Open call: Women's Economic and Leadership Opportunities Fund 2026

**Deadline: 15 September, 12:00 noon Pacific.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Local reach | up to **$625,000** |
| Regional / provincial / interprovincial | up to **$1,000,000** |
| Pan-Canadian | up to **$1,400,000** |
| Northern / remote uplift | additional **35%** |
| Project duration | up to **60 months** |
| Administration | 15–20% of total requested |

Pan-Canadian requires impact in **at least four WAGE regions** and delivery in
**both official languages** (waivable for Indigenous organizations).

### Goal

"To improve women's access to leadership and economic opportunities." Projects
must address root causes through **systemic change**, not individual adaptation.

### Priorities

1. Women's access to leadership positions across public and private sectors
2. Women in STEM, trades and traditionally male-dominated fields
3. Wage gaps and concentration of women in lower-paid roles
4. **Opportunities for women in an AI-driven economy**

### The five systemic change approaches

Applications are built around these:

1. Changing gender norms and attitudes
2. Supporting shifts in authority and decision-making power
3. Increasing networks and collaboration
4. Redistributing resources
5. Modifying policies and practices

## ⚠ The constraint that will trip ACBN

**Service delivery, capacity building, scaling, and fees to participants are all
INELIGIBLE expenses.**

This inverts ACBN's usual application. Accelerators, training cohorts,
mentorship programs, microloans and participant stipends — the things ACBN
builds by instinct and has been funded for by FedDev and SBCCI — **cannot be
funded here**.

WAGE is buying change in *institutions*, not services for individuals. Every
activity must act on a system: a lender's adjudication criteria, an employer's
promotion practice, a board's nomination rules, a sector's norms.

If a draft describes what participants will receive, it is wrong. It must
describe what an institution will do differently.

## Eligibility — ACBN qualifies

- Canadian organization ✓
- Not-for-profit ✓
- Legally constituted with proof of incorporation ✓ (ONCA, OCN 1987142)
- **No charitable registration required** — the constraint in
  `../library/eligibility.md` does not bite here
- "Experience in advancing equality for women" — ACBN's case rests on a
  constituency that is 74% women (see `../library/reach-and-impact.md`)

## ⚠ The competitive risk

**The fund prioritizes women's organizations and Indigenous women's
organizations.** ACBN is a Black business network whose constituency is majority
women — not a women's organization.

That is a real disadvantage as lead applicant, and it is worth deciding
deliberately whether ACBN leads or partners with a Black women's organization
that leads. See the strategy note in
`../applications/wage-womens-economic-leadership-2026.md`.
