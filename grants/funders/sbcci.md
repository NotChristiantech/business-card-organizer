# SBCCI

Capacity-building funding for grassroots not-for-profits serving Black
communities in Canada.

## Our history — funded twice

| Award | Year | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Capacity Building | 2020 | $100,000 |
| Capacity Building | 2024 | $40,000 |

Plus **$21,000 secured for the New Life Project** on their behalf.

Submission record: `https://sbcc-acnc.smapply.ca/sub/30176832/`

**Being funded twice is the credential** — a repeat award means the first was
delivered and reported acceptably. Combined with having brokered SBCCI money to
another organization, it is a strong, specific claim for any capacity-building
program.

## Worth understanding: why the second award was smaller

$100,000 down to $40,000. Most likely a smaller envelope, a different stream, or
a smaller ask — but if it reflects assessment of the first award, that matters
for every federal renewal ACBN pursues, BEP 2.0 included. Cheap to ask, and the
answer generalises.
