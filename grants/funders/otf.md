## Why it's worth real attention

OTF is the largest provincial community funder in Ontario, runs several streams
per year, and funds general operating and capacity work that federal project
funding won't touch — which is precisely the money that is hardest for ACBN to
raise. A live OTF relationship is a durable asset, not a one-off.

An external sector peer volunteering that the questions fit is a meaningfully
better signal than a funding digest hit.
